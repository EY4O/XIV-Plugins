Features

Everything your next craft needs, in one list.

  • Shopping List. Add items by search, from the right-click menu, or by pasting a Teamcraft list.
  • Need and have. Counts what you hold across your bags, crystals, saddlebag and retainers.
  • Prices. The cheapest worlds on your data center, NPC vendors that sell for less, and what finishing the list would cost.
  • Retainers. Each retainer's gil and listings, and which listings have been undercut.

Available now

Check your bags before you shop.

Add Looneth's repository, then install Bagcheck from /xlplugins. A short welcome guide opens the first time, and /bagcheck opens it any time after.

Good to know
