Features

Every look you've made, one click away.

  • Save your plate. The portrait's pose, expression, camera, lighting, background, frame and accent, and the design's base plate, borders, decorations and layout.
  • Restore. Opens the plate's own editors, puts it all back and checks it, then leaves it for you to look over and save. Parts that already match are skipped.
  • Gear set portraits. Save any gear set's portrait into a gallery, and put one on several gear sets. Each opens in turn for you to look over and save; anything a job can't use keeps what that gear set has.
  • Share. Copy a plate as a short code and import other players' plates. Anything you haven't unlocked keeps your own choice.
  • Pictures and favourites. Capture and crop a picture for each plate and portrait, and pin the ones you use most to the top.
  • Backups. Export your plates and portraits, pictures included, as one file, and import them on a new PC or another character.

Good to know

Restore only uses what your character has unlocked, and names anything it had to leave out.

Saving is yours by default. Settings can let RePlate press Save once it has checked the result.
