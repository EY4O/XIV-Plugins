Selling

Your retainers, repriced in one pass.

Undercutting by hand gets old fast. With AutoTataru you open the summoning bell and press Sell All. It visits each retainer, prices every listing and reprices anything that has been undercut.

  • Prices from real sales. It uses the median of recent sales from Universalis, so one listing at 1 gil or at 9,999,999 gil can't move your price.
  • You set the lowest price. Each item gets a minimum it will never sell below. You choose the undercut, either a flat amount or a percentage.
  • HQ and NQ priced separately. It knows the difference, even when one of them has little sales history.
  • Works with AutoRetainer. If AutoRetainer already runs your ventures, AutoTataru can reprice during the same retainer visit.
  • Add items from the right-click menu. Right-click an item in your inventory to add it to the sell list.

Buying

A shopping list that shops for you.

Paste a crafting list. It works out what you already have and buys only what you still need, at a fair price, on whichever world sells it cheapest.

  • Imports from Teamcraft. Paste a Teamcraft list and AutoTataru picks out the raw materials. Each import becomes its own named group that you can switch on or off.
  • Buys across your data center. It scans every world, shows you the route, and after you approve it, travels and buys. Travel uses Lifestream and vnavmesh.
  • Counts what you already own. Bags, crystals, saddlebag and retainers all count, so it won't buy materials you already have.
  • Limits set for you. AutoTataru suggests a price ceiling and budget from market history. You can type your own values to override them.
  • Checks NPC vendors. If a vendor sells the item for less, the run won't pay more for it on the board.
  • Keep in stock. Pick a quantity for the materials you always use, and each run tops you back up to that amount.

Control

You're always in control.

Automation you can't see into makes people nervous, so AutoTataru shows its work.

  • One-click STOP. The run halts immediately, from the header or with /taru stop.
  • Stops by itself when something looks wrong. If a game window shows something it didn't expect, it halts instead of guessing.
  • Logs every decision. Each sale and purchase records what it did and why: the median it used, the undercut it applied and the floor it respected.
  • Imports start switched off. Items imported from Teamcraft can't be bought until they have limits.
