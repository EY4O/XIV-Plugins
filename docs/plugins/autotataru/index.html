Selling

Your retainers, repriced in one pass.

Undercutting by hand gets old fast. With AutoTataru you open the summoning bell and press Sell All. It visits each retainer in turn, lists what your Sell List says to sell, and reprices anything that has been undercut.

  • Prices from the board itself. For each item it opens the game's own price comparison and reads the live listings and recent sales. Universalis history backs it up when the game shows too few sales.
  • Hard to fool. It works from the median of recent sales. Asks far below it are treated as bait and ignored, and a price never climbs further above the median than you allow.
  • You set the lowest price. Each item has a minimum it will never sell below, prefilled from recent market sales for you to review. The undercut is yours too: a flat amount (one gil by default) or a percentage, for every item or just one.
  • Your own listings are safe. It won't undercut your other retainers, and it only reprices when the change is big enough and the listing has had time to sell.
  • HQ and NQ priced separately. It knows the difference, even when one of them has little sales history.
  • Works with AutoRetainer, or on a timer. It can sell during AutoRetainer's retainer visits, or repeat Sell All on a schedule.
  • Easy to add items. Right-click an item in your inventory, or use the hover hotkey. Items it finds already listed on a retainer are added switched off, for you to review.

Buying

A shopping list that shops for you.

Paste a crafting list. It works out what you already have and buys only what you still need, at a fair price, on whichever world sells it cheapest.

  • Imports from Teamcraft. Paste a Teamcraft list and AutoTataru picks out the raw materials. Each import becomes its own named group that you can switch on or off, or re-import to update.
  • Counts what you already own. Bags, crystals and saddlebag, plus your retainers as of your last visit, so it won't buy materials you already have.
  • Limits set for you. Each item's price ceiling comes from the median of recent sales on your data center plus a margin you choose, and its budget covers what you still need. Type your own values to override them.
  • Buy once, or keep in stock. Buy a set amount, or pick a quantity for the materials you always use and each run tops you back up.
  • Scan, then approve the route. It checks every world's prices, shows you the route and skips worlds that don't save enough to be worth the trip. Nothing moves until you approve it.
  • Travels for you. It leaves from a market city, visits each world with Lifestream, walks to the board with vnavmesh, and can take you home afterwards.
  • Checks NPC vendors. The Buy List shows which NPCs sell an item and where. If a vendor sells it for less, the run won't pay more for it on the board unless you allow it.
  • Buys through the game's own board. It searches, checks the listing is still exactly what it planned, and confirms only a purchase that states the expected item, quantity and price.

Control

You're always in control.

Automation you can't see into makes people nervous, so AutoTataru shows its work.

  • Nothing runs until you turn it on. Selling and buying both start switched off.
  • One-click STOP. The run halts immediately, from any AutoTataru window, with a hotkey, or with /taru stop.
  • Stops by itself when something looks wrong. If a game window shows something it didn't expect, it halts instead of guessing.
  • Spend caps everywhere. A maximum per run and per item, plus a price ceiling on every purchase.
  • Never "no limit" by accident. New and imported items get automatic limits. An item with too few recent sales isn't bought until you set a limit yourself, or an NPC's price caps it.
  • Logs every decision. Each sale and purchase records what it did and why: the median it used, the undercut it applied and the limit it respected.
  • Everything in view. See each retainer's gil, slots and listings against the cheapest competitor, and keep your sale history for as many days as you like.
